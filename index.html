Owner setup · connect the other free services

These three providers need their own verified destination before they can send mail. Use davinderpalbrar401@gmail.com, choose the free plan, and enable email notifications.

  1. Web3Forms: verify your email, create a form, and copy its public access key.
  2. Formspree: create a free account and a form, confirm the recipient, and copy the form endpoint.
  3. Formspark: create a free account and a form, turn on email notifications, and copy its action URL.

Send those three public form identifiers to the person updating this site, or add them to forms-config.js in the repository and publish the change. Use the Formspree and Formspark IDs from the end of their URLs. Never use an account password or private API key.

After connecting a provider, send one test and check the inbox and spam folder. A provider accepting a submission does not prove that its email arrived.

Free-plan information checked 11 October 2026. Limits are shared with other forms on the same provider account where applicable and may change. Follow the provider links for current details.
